We make getting in the easy part

AdmitOne started with a simple frustration: great events were being held back by clumsy ticketing.

Our story

Organizers were juggling spreadsheets, bank transfers and paper tickets. Attendees were hunting through chats for a screenshot of a receipt.

So we built one place to list an event, sell seats and check people in. It works the same for a 40-person workshop and a 4,000-person festival, and it keeps the price honest from the first click to the door.

What we stand for

Honest prices

The price you see on the event page is the price you pay. No surprise fees at checkout.

Fast at the door

Every ticket is a QR code that scans in a second, so queues stay short.

Fair to organizers

Small per-ticket fees, quick payouts and automatic refunds when plans change.
