Questions about pricing

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

Do attendees pay anything extra?

No. The price on the event page is the price at checkout. Per-ticket fees are paid by the organizer.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply from your next billing date.

When do I get paid for ticket sales?

Payouts are sent two working days after your event ends, minus the per-ticket fee.

What happens if I cancel an event?

Every ticket holder is refunded automatically and we waive the per-ticket fee.

Is there a fee for free events?

No. Free events carry no per-ticket fee on any plan.
